# Trading + Gann Question Bank

This bank defines the core questions the system and trader should ask before execution, during execution, and after execution.

## Regime Questions
1. What is the dominant market regime now: trend, range, transition, or trap?
2. Is this regime stable across major and minor timeframes?

## Risk Questions
1. Is a high-impact event guard active and blocking directional execution?
2. What is current reliability score vs threshold for valid execution?

## Structure (SMC) Questions
1. Is BOS/CHOCH confirmed in the intended trade direction?
2. Are HH/HL or LL/LH sequences aligned with entry direction?

## Market Physics Questions
1. Is momentum strengthening, weakening, or diverging from structure?
2. Is acceleration supporting continuation or signaling exhaustion?

## Gann Questions
1. Has price reached a cardinal/key Gann angle (45/90/180/225/315)?
2. Is Gann angle proximity EXACT/NEAR/NONE at the current bar?
3. Is Price=Time relationship aligned enough for execution now?
4. What is the nearest key angle, and does it act as launch or rejection level?

## Time / Cycle Questions
1. Is the selected date inside an active signal time window?
2. Is the setup early, on-time, or late relative to cycle phase?

## Numerology Questions
1. Are event, date, and price numerology harmoniously aligned?
2. What numerology cycle phase is active: expansion, consolidation, or completion?

## Astrology Questions
1. What nearby astro event is active and what impact level is expected?
2. Does observed market behavior match astro narration expectations?

## Confluence Questions
1. Do geometry, time, structure, and tape all confirm together?
2. What is the final confluence verdict: BUY, SELL, or WAIT?

## Execution Questions
1. What are the exact entry, stop, target, and expected hold window?
2. Is projected move sufficient after spread/slippage and risk costs?

## AI Learning Questions
1. How similar is this setup to past winning/losing patterns in memory?
2. Is model confidence calibrated for this regime or drifting?

## Post-Trade Questions
1. Did realized move match projected direction, magnitude, and time window?
2. Which concept failed first when a setup was wrong: geometry, time, structure, or tape?

## Gann Mindset Narrative Template
Price has reached a cardinal angle near {nearest_angle}deg (current {current_degree}deg), and time is in phase with {cycle_phase} during active window {start_time} to {end_time}. Structure has {structure_state}, and {tape_state} (momentum={momentum}). This is not random movement; this is organized {direction}. Start price {start_price}, target zone {end_price}. Therefore: {bias} continuation bias, not blind chase.
